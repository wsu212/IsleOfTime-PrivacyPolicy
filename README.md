# Privacy Policy for Isle of Time

**Effective Date:** October 29, 2026

Isle of Time ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the Isle of Time app for iPhone, iPad and Mac collects, why, and what we do with it.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect information only when you choose to add your name to the supporters wall (section 3). Otherwise the app sends nothing about you to us.

* **Reading progress, bookmarks and settings** stay on your device. We never receive them.
* **Content and audio** are downloaded from our servers. These requests do not carry any account or identifier.
* **We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking services.**

## 2. Purchases

Tips are processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details, Apple ID, name or email. The app reads your own purchase history from Apple on your device so that it can recognise you as a supporter.

## 3. Supporters Wall

If you have bought a tip, the app may ask whether you would like your name on the supporters wall. This is optional. If you agree, the app sends:

* the name you enter (if you choose to stay anonymous, no name is sent);
* an identifier generated on your device, used only to mark your own name on the wall;
* Apple's signed record of that purchase (product, date and transaction number), used to confirm the support is genuine.

Names are reviewed by hand before they appear publicly. The wall shows names only, never amounts. Submissions are stored with our content management provider, Sanity, and the published wall is hosted on GitHub.

To have your name removed or corrected, email us at the address below.

## 4. Sharing Information

We do not sell, trade or share your information with third parties, apart from the service providers named in section 3 that store it for us.

## 5. Data Security

Submissions travel over encrypted connections, and only we can read or edit them.

## 6. Children's Privacy

Isle of Time is available for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without parental consent. A parent who believes their child has submitted a name can ask us to remove it.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

## 8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, or want your name removed from the supporters wall, please contact us at:
`wsu212@gmail.com`

# 海上的島 隱私權政策

**生效日期：** 2026 年 10 月 29 日

## 1. 我們收集的資料

只有在你選擇把名字寫上感謝名單時（第 3 節），我們才會收到資料。除此之外，App 不會把任何關於你的資料傳給我們。

* **閱讀進度、書籤與設定**只存在你的裝置上，我們收不到。
* **內容與音檔**從我們的伺服器下載，請求裡不帶任何帳號或識別碼。
* **我們不使用分析、廣告或追蹤服務。**

## 2. 購買

贊助的付款完全由 Apple 處理，我們看不到你的付款資料、Apple ID、姓名或電子郵件。App 會在你的裝置上向 Apple 讀取你自己的購買紀錄，用來認出你贊助過。

## 3. 感謝名單

贊助過的讀者，App 可能會詢問是否要把名字寫上感謝名單，這完全是自願的。同意的話，App 會送出：

* 你填寫的名字（選擇不具名就不會送出名字）；
* 你的裝置產生的識別碼，只用來在名單上標出你自己的名字；
* Apple 簽署的那筆贊助紀錄（品項、日期與交易編號），用來確認贊助屬實。

名字經人工審核後才會公開，名單只列名字，不顯示金額。送出的資料存放在我們使用的內容管理服務 Sanity，公開的名單放在 GitHub 上。

想撤下或更正名字，請寄信到下方信箱。

## 4. 資料分享

我們不出售、交換或分享你的資料給第三方；第 3 節提到、替我們存放資料的服務商除外。

## 5. 資料安全

送出的資料經加密連線傳送，只有我們能讀取或編輯。

## 6. 兒童隱私

海上的島適合所有年齡的讀者。我們不會在未經家長同意下刻意收集兒童的個人資料。家長若認為孩子送出了名字，可以來信要求撤下。

## 7. 政策變更

本政策可能不定期更新，更新後的版本會公布在本頁，並標上新的生效日期。

## 8. 聯絡我們

對本政策有任何疑問，或想撤下感謝名單上的名字，請來信：
`wsu212@gmail.com`
